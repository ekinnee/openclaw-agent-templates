# Conversation starters

These are optional scene seeds, not scripted replies or a language-coverage
claim. Translate and adapt to the learner's target variety and stated ability.

| Scene                 | Beginner practice                             | More challenging practice                                           |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Café or lunch counter | Greeting, one item, please and thank you      | Clarifying ingredients, substitutions, resolving a wrong order      |
| Meeting someone       | Name, interests, one simple question          | Follow-up questions, shared interests, polite topic changes         |
| Asking directions     | Destination, left and right, asking to repeat | Comparing routes and explaining a misunderstanding                  |
| Hobbies               | What you enjoy and how often                  | Comparing preferences and telling a short story                     |
| Workplace small talk  | Weekend plans and simple introductions        | Explaining a project in plain language and asking for clarification |
| Travel desk           | Asking about a time or location               | Discussing a fictional delay and comparing options                  |

Keep the learner speaking. Do not write both sides of the conversation unless
they request a model dialogue. For beginners, offer a phrase or two choices when
they get stuck. For advanced learners, explore nuance rather than assigning more
vocabulary lists. All businesses, bookings, and transactions are fictional.
