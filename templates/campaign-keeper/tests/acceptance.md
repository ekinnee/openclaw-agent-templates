# Acceptance scenarios — design checks, not observed tests

The engine and approved multiplayer channel do not exist in this template directory. These are eventual acceptance cases, not evidence of implementation. A model-only playthrough cannot pass engine tests.

1. From a fresh install, a solo player takes the Quay Runner; first response offers tone preferences briefly and starts at Lantern Quay. The opening player projection contains visible Quay details and exits, not the safety line, Archive plan or endings. An unavailable model does not prevent deterministic look/move/inventory commands.
2. Two distinct authenticated senders join the **same approved conversation binding** with separate characters and move to different rooms. A self-asserted owner name, wrong-room item claim, cross-campaign access and another player's character action are rejected at the engine state boundary. Two tabs of one account are not distinct players.
3. Two players race for the unique prism or mirror: one receives a committed claim, the other a clear non-success. Retrying the first stable action ID returns the same receipt and dice with no new effect; authorization is still checked. An uncertain response is reconciled before retrying.
4. Two present Pump players use held-lever intention and wheel action asynchronously; holder can cancel. A solo player can recruit Suri in Tide Market or brace with the crate. An unbraced rush opens on every band; verify the selected mixed-band cost, failure surge or capped Resolve loss, zero-Resolve retreat, and replay of the committed result. Tower stair is an independent route. No failed roll removes an essential clue.
5. Tavern plate, Market shell/slate plus Pump diagram, Archive plan and Mara each enable comprehension of the Tower procedure without a single unique item gate. A failed clue roll still reveals the essential clue. Prism, mirror and improvised reflector each permit a balanced ending.
6. Resolve reaches zero after an effect: effect is committed, character retreats, unique item and learned clue remain, recovery reaches two. Surge reaches two: every room and ending remains available. Inactivity does not raise surge.
7. Play balanced, diverted, shuttered and explicitly deferred endings in separate campaigns. Players—not GM/NPC—choose; a pause does not commit an ending; an ending cannot silently rewrite on replay.
8. Restart the owned disposable test context: characters, items, world flags, journal, dice receipts and campaign binding persist. Unreadable state does not reset silently. Failure of narration delivery cannot duplicate a committed action.
9. Inspect player text, exports and any board DOM/JSON for unrevealed GM notes or other characters' private inventory; an old snapshot visibly declares its version and has equivalent text. Host owner file access is not a player secrecy boundary.
10. After a real immutable plugin release, validate/dev/build twice against unchanged source and compare archive bytes; inspect grant profile and exact dependency, then isolated Add and real two-identity channel operations. Until then label portable Add blocked.
11. Offer an unscripted but plausible solo intent, such as tying the discovered safety line to the Pump lever. The GM distinguishes intent from outcome, checks the actual carried item and location, maps to `/ck brace pump-lever safety-line <id>`, and narrates only the committed receipt. Replaying that ID makes no second effect. A proposed untracked rope or an unsupported world-changing action receives clarification or a supported alternative, never a fabricated saved fact. Observe this with a real GM/model turn; command fixtures alone do not satisfy it.
12. Pause after an unresolved scene and resume later: no idle-time surge, automatic turn, NPC decision, or deferred ending is committed. Verify the same committed facts and pending player choice after a real isolated Gateway restart. A later two-character demonstration requires independently authenticated participants, distinct priorities, a negotiated shared obstacle and a binding-current SDK guard; one owner with two tabs or synthetic contexts is not sufficient.
